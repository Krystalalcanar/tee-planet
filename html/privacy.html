Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Oct. 17, 2026

At Tee Planet, your privacy matters to us.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly.

When you use Tee Planet, we may collect the following information:

  • Order Information: Details about the products you purchase, your selected sizes, and your order history.

We use your information to process orders, respond to inquiries, improve our website, and provide updates about our products.

Your Privacy Rights

Policy Updates
