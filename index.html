About Our Startup

Tee Planet started with a simple idea: everyday clothes should be comfortable, stylish, and affordable.

We created this store for students and young adults who want great-looking graphic tees without paying crazy prices.

Whether you're heading to class, hanging out with friends, or just relaxing at home, our T-shirts are made to fit right into your day. We believe looking good shouldn't be complicated - it should just feel like you.

How We Began

  1. December 2024 The idea of a T-shirt company was born.
  2. January 2025 We brainstormed designs and materials.
  3. April 2025 We gathered funding for Tee Planet.
  4. January 2026 We finalized the branding and product testing.
  5. October 2026 We released our first collection and launched our new website.

Mission

To offer comfortable, affordable T-shirts that help people express their personality through everyday style.

We create designs that reflect different interests, moods, and stories.

Through friendly service and thoughtful design, we make it easy for everyone to "Wear Your World."

Vision

To become a go-to T-shirt brand that celebrates individuality and creativity.

We aspire to build a community where people feel confident expressing themselves through what they wear.

As we grow, we aim to keep comfort, affordability, and meaningful design at the heart of Tee Planet.
